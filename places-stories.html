Field Notes · BERAE in Practice

Training, site work, and partnerships across the Calamianes.

These field notes document selected RnK activities that connect BERAE with schools, farmers, community partners, and local institutions. Each entry reflects the activity documented in the supplied project archive without treating every site visit or training as a completed long-term outcome.

November 12–13, 2025 · BERAE Training

From classroom discussion to field learning.

RnK-SGP conducted a two-day BERAE training, with November 13 dedicated to field-based learning. The activity connected the BERAE framework with practical observation and discussion on the ground.

November 19, 2025 · Salvacion National High School

Learning water-efficient irrigation by doing.

RnK supported drip irrigation training and installation at Salvacion National High School with Mr. Jim Hunt, combining practical instruction with hands-on field work.

January 22–23, 2026 · Coron & Busuanga

Training K–12 teachers and farmers around BERAE.

RnK brought together K–12 teachers and farmers from Coron and Busuanga for SHGBEE and BERAE learning that combined group sessions with field observation and practical site-based discussion.

January 30, 2026 · WPU–RAC

Drip irrigation training with farmers.

At the WPU–RAC site, farmers and participants joined hands-on drip irrigation training as part of RnK's practical work around regenerative agriculture and careful water use.

March 29, 2026 · San Nicolas Elementary School

Installing drip irrigation for a school garden.

RnK documented the installation of drip irrigation at San Nicolas Elementary School, adding a practical water-management component to the school's garden setting.

August 5, 2026 · Cheey, Busuanga

Good Seeds, Good Harvests with CAFA farmers.

RnK conducted seed training for farmers of the Cheey Aquaculture and Fisherfolk Association (CAFA) with former SEARCA Director and seed scientist Dr. Glenn Gregorio.

August 5, 2026 · Busuanga

Sharing RnK's vision and introducing BERAE.

RnK made courtesy visits to Busuanga Mayor RC Cruz and WPU–Busuanga Campus Director Bea Cruz-Vicera to share the Foundation's projects and vision and to introduce BERAE.

2026 · Cheey, Busuanga

A community site for practicing BERAE.

CAFA is an RnK community partner in Barangay Cheey, Busuanga. The association provided a site where BERAE can be practiced and implemented, with 2026 field documentation showing land and site preparation.

Kilit Festival

An annual celebration of biodiversity and community.

The Kilit Festival is an annual community event that brings people together around biodiversity awareness, education, culture, creativity, and community participation in the Calamianes. During the 2025 festival week in Malbato, berae was introduced publicly during RnK-SGP training on November 12, followed by the Sixth Kilit Festival on November 14. RnK source materials estimate that the festival drew around 1,000 attendees, predominantly K–12 students and accompanying adults from the community.
