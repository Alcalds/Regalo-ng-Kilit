# RnK Multi-page V1.6 — Public Content Audit

This pass removes administrative, internal-development, redundant, or ambiguous wording that does not help a normal website visitor.

## Removed from public-facing About
- SEC registration number
- Exact SEC registration date
- President from the At-a-Glance facts (already identified beside the founder portrait)

The At-a-Glance section now keeps only three visitor-relevant facts: Established, Organization type, and Geographic Focus.

## Rewritten across the site
- Internal phrases such as “staff-supplied,” “source materials,” “latest staff materials,” “confirmed public channels,” “appear in the record,” and similar development/research language.
- About timeline wording is now visitor-facing instead of describing the source documents.
- BERAE and SHGBEE are explained directly rather than as material extracted from staff files.
- Places & Stories now presents Coron and Busuanga as the current geographic focus shown on the website; Culion and Linapacan were removed from the current “Where We Work” list because the supplied materials support them as broader organizational objectives, not equally documented current website locations.
- Scholar and biodiversity introductions now speak directly to visitors.
- Kilit Festival copy is framed as documented history, without implying a current 2025/2026 festival schedule.
- Get Involved avoids backend/process language and expands “LGUs” for general visitors.
- Partner copy is framed as historical/established collaboration rather than “materials reflect…” language.

## Deliberately retained
- Established 2019
- Philippine NGO identity
- Coron, Palawan geographic focus
- Dr. Manuel R. Reyes as President beside his portrait
- Official Vision and Mission
- Verified historical milestones
- BERAE and SHGBEE explanations
- Staff-provided scholar stories, biodiversity images, public email, and Facebook link

## Still intentionally omitted
- Bank information
- Personal phone numbers and personal email addresses
- Exact sensitive coordinates
- Grant budget figures and internal monitoring details
- Unverified impact counters / beneficiary totals
- Donation, online payment, sponsorship, or formal volunteer workflows that RnK has not yet confirmed
