# RnK Hero Concept C — Editorial Chapters V1

## Design intention
A more formal alternative to the Living System orbit and Kilit Lens concepts. The full-screen photography remains the dominant visual. Interaction is intentionally restrained.

## What changes by chapter
Only three things change:
1. the full-bleed background photograph,
2. the emphasized line of the approved RnK tagline,
3. one short supporting sentence.

## Chapters
1. Biodiversity
2. Food
3. Landscape & Tourism
4. Community & Livelihoods

## What was deliberately removed
- no circular orbit,
- no central bird graphic,
- no floating cards,
- no lens,
- no current-chapter panel,
- no duplicate instructional labels.

## Interaction
The four chapters sit in one quiet editorial rail along the bottom of the hero. Clicking a chapter cross-fades the background. A slow 9-second autoplay is retained so the hero still feels alive, but it pauses while the visitor hovers or focuses within the hero. Reduced-motion preferences disable autoplay transitions.

## Content rules
The staff-provided tagline remains unchanged:

> Enhancing biodiversity while growing food, boosting tourism, and increasing income.

The supporting copy is concise and does not introduce impact statistics or grant targets as achievements.


## V1.1 sequence refinement
The third and fourth scenes were swapped so the visual chapter progression now follows the approved tagline in direct order: Biodiversity → Food → Tourism → Income/Livelihoods.
