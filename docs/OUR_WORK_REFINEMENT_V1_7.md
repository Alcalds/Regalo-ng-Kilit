# RnK Our Work — Refinement V1.7

This pass responds to the desktop review of the Our Work page.

## Main changes
- Reduced the five equal program cards to four primary work areas.
- Moved Knowledge & Partnerships from a stand-alone work area into an across-every-area note, avoiding overlap with About / partners content.
- Removed the embedded BERAE poster from the public page. The poster is valuable source material, but its dense text is not legible enough as a web section and visually competed with the page.
- Rebuilt BERAE as a web-native explanation with three connected dimensions.
- Kept a real RnK garden photograph as the visual anchor for BERAE.
- Simplified SHGBEE into a clearer School → Home → Community learning pathway.
- Reorganized the seven BERAE sites by role: schools, higher education, community organization, and conservation landscape.
- Tightened section heights and reduced repeated box/card patterns.

## Public-content guardrails retained
- No proposal targets are presented as achieved impact.
- No grant budgets, internal monitoring details, personal contact data, or exact wildlife coordinates are shown.
- The seven BERAE sites remain based on staff-supplied site materials.
