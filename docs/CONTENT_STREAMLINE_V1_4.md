# RnK Multi-page V1.4 — Content Streamline

## Purpose
Reduce repeated material between the homepage and the dedicated About, Our Work, and Places & Stories pages.

## Homepage changes
- Why Regalo ng Kilit is now a concise origin-story preview; the founder quotation and full narrative remain on About.
- Our Work is now a four-area overview; detailed BERAE framework, SHGBEE explanation, and seven-site network remain on Our Work.
- The full biodiversity gallery and full scholar carousel were removed from Home and combined into a compact “From the Calamianes” three-card preview.
- Partners and Get Involved remain concise trust/conversion sections.

## Interior-page changes
- Removed the duplicate Education & Scholars feature from Our Work because Education already appears among the five work areas and full scholar stories live under Places & Stories.
- Added stable anchors for the BERAE Network, Biodiversity, Scholars, and Kilit Festival sections.
- Removed prototype-only process disclaimers from the public-facing Get Involved page; the page remains inquiry-based and does not invent donation/volunteer mechanisms.
