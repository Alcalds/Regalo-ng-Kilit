# RnK Multi-page V1.2 — Homepage Section 2 Fix

Visual review identified two problems in the `Why Regalo ng Kilit?` block:

1. The fixed navigation could cover the section heading and the top of the founder portrait while the visitor scrolled down.
2. The decorative Kilit illustration could overlap the founder name/caption.

Changes:
- Header now hides while scrolling down and returns when scrolling up, near the top, or when the mobile menu is open.
- Existing compact-header state is retained.
- Founder text and portrait remain top-aligned.
- Decorative Kilit is smaller and placed partly behind the portrait, below the caption layer.
- Responsive offsets were adjusted for 1100px, 980px, and 720px breakpoints.

No website copy, facts, navigation labels, Hero chapter sequence, or staff-supplied content were changed.
